## 1. Phrase-binding — design and feasibility

jimy-r replied in [#584](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/discussions/584)
that a phrase→skill table with a standing rule fixed activation on his roster,
and shared a subset. Arm A: that rule verbatim + one row per skill in **our**
cases' wording; arm B: nothing. v3 suite, assay 0.4.4.

- **As first specified (table in the isolated `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`) it was not
  possible on 0.4.4**: the adapter `mkdtemp`s an empty config dir per attempt,
  spawns, deletes it; no flag or env reaches it. Stopped and said so.
- **jimy-r's table is in the *workspace* `CLAUDE.md`** (project scope), not the
  user one. That scope is reachable with no code: every workdir is
  `mkdtemp(os.tmpdir())` and `TEMP` is on the env allowlist, so the table at
  `<arm>/CLAUDE.md` with `TEMP=<arm>/tmp` is an ancestor of every attempt. User
  chose this. Smoke through the real adapter: canary → `HELIOTROPE-7`, control
  → `NONE`.
- Table: `fixtures/phrase-binding/CLAUDE.md`, 34 phrases, 32 verbatim from the
  case they route; script-checked that none occurs in another case or a
  negative. Analyser `tools/phrase_binding.py` on top of `collide.py`, validated
  against `912ad216` before any new data.
