# Brief — Quarrylane (fictional test brief)

Quarrylane is log search for small engineering teams: point it at your log
files, type a query, get results in under a second. No cluster to run.

Audience: backend engineers at companies of 5 to 50 people who find the big
observability suites too heavy.

The page needs: what it is, how it works in three steps, what it costs (free
for one project, 20 a month per team after that), and a way to start.
