# Brief — Crumbkiln Bakery (fictional test brief)

A neighbourhood bakery with a wood-fired oven. Sourdough, seeded rye,
morning buns on weekends. Open Tuesday to Sunday, 7am until sold out.

Audience: people who live within a few streets and want to know what is
baked today and when to come.

The page needs: who they are, what they bake, opening hours, where they are,
and how to order a whole loaf ahead.
