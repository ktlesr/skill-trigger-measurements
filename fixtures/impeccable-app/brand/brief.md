# Meterly — wordmark brief

Meterly meters API usage per second and bills against it. The name is read
aloud as *meter-ly*, and the product's whole promise is that the number you see
is the number you are charged.

- Applications: the mark alone at 24px favicon size, the wordmark on a trade
  stand banner at 3m, and both together on invoice stationery.
- Reproduction: single colour on white, single colour reversed, and one
  two-colour version. It has to survive a one-colour rubber stamp.
- Avoid: the speedometer needle, the lightning bolt, the bar chart. Every
  competitor in this category has one.
- Existing: nothing. The current wordmark is Helvetica typed out in a slide.
