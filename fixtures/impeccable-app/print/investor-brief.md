# Meterly — printed investor update, Q3

A 16-page saddle-stitched booklet, 200 copies, litho on 120gsm uncoated stock.
There is no web version of this document and there will not be one.

## Figures that have to carry the pages

- Net revenue retention by cohort, eight quarterly cohorts.
- Gross margin split into infrastructure, support and payment fees.
- Metered call volume against billed volume, monthly, 24 months.
- Customer count by plan tier, four tiers.

## Constraints from the printer

- CMYK only. No spot inks in the budget, no fluorescents.
- Uncoated stock: expect 18–22% dot gain in the midtones and a maximum total
  ink coverage of 280%.
- Two of the 200 copies get photocopied in black and white by the auditors,
  so every figure must still read without colour.
- Body text is set in the house serif at 9.5/13.5. The figures sit inside that
  measure, 132mm.
